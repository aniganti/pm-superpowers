## Skill gaps still visible

The set passes. These are what a faithful run still cannot say cleanly.

1. **Two "strongest" answers.** Costco's strongest scored moat is the membership loop. The required lead says economies of scale may be the strongest. Atlassian's strongest scored moat is switching costs; the lead says ecosystem. Visa's strongest scored moat is the network; the lead says brand. The lead is what the re-run rule grades. A reader who stops at the scorecard still leaves with the secondary moat.

2. **Learner scale has no type of its own.** Duolingo's filing lists "the scale, growth and engagement of our learners" as a competitive factor. The only screen bucket is economies of scale, which is a cost moat. This run marked it Yes and said the evidence is reach. A later run can mark the same fact No, call it reach rather than cost, and still satisfy the company file. The type name and the filing's word are not the same thing.

3. **The opening Item 1A heading can lose to the substitute.** Visa's first risk heading is "We are subject to complex and evolving global regulations that could harm our business and financial results." Both passes screen regulatory Yes and then name network effects as most-at-risk, because the intake's substitute is account-to-account payments and that threat sits under "We face intense competition in our industry." C4 is a 2. A reader of only the most-at-risk bullet does not see that regulation opens the risk factors. The skill still has no risk flag beside a rating. This run did not invent one.

4. **The frozen intake has no substitute for three companies.** Atlassian, Costco, and Intuitive named rivals only. Public-company mode filled the substitute from Item 1 (general-purpose AI assistants, online comparison shopping, remanufactured instruments). A private company with that same intake would finish with the gap flagged and the copy-risk bullet short.

5. **One source tag drops the second fact.** Several moats are a product sentence in Item 1 and a threat in Item 1A. The evidence line can carry one tag, so the cross-check repeats the heading instead of the row carrying both.
