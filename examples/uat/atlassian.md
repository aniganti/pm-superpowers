## Hypothesized primary moats

Integrated Cloud platform and Marketplace ecosystem; Teamwork Graph context; product-led scale. Switching costs are real (most revenue from existing accounts) and are not the filing's lead claim.

**MD&A (context, not Item 1).** "more than 350,000 customers." Over 90% of FY2026 revenue growth came from accounts that existed on or before 2025-06-30.
