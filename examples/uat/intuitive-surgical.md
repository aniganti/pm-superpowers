## Hypothesized primary moats

Ecosystem: robotic systems, proprietary instruments and accessories, training, and service, tied together by a digital portfolio. Regulatory and IP barriers: FDA clearance and a large patent estate. Network effects are not the moat the business depends on.

## Quotes used

**Item 1 — ecosystem, in the company's words.** "providing a comprehensive ecosystem that includes robotic-assisted systems, instruments and accessories, customer learning, and customer support services all connected by a digital portfolio."

**Item 1 — IP.** "As of December 31, 2025, we owned more than 5,600 patents granted and still in force and more than 2,500 patents pending worldwide."

**Item 1 — regulation.** Current products are subject to premarket notification and clearance under section 510(k).

**Item 1 — competitors.** The named set includes Johnson & Johnson, Medtronic plc, CMR Surgical, Distalmotion, Karl Storz, Medicaroid, and several China-based system makers (including Beijing Surgerii Robotics, Shanghai Microport Medbot, Shenzhen Edge Medical, and SS Innovations). The filing expects increasing competition within China.

**Item 1A — remanufactured instruments.** "Third parties may offer to sell remanufactured or unauthorized instruments and accessories to our customers or provide unauthorized service on our systems." Antitrust claims from parties that want to compete in instruments or servicing are in the same risk family.

**Item 1A — other threats to the moat.** Tariffs on instruments manufactured in Mexico; Chinese provincial limits on what hospitals can charge for robotic surgery; FDA disruption that could delay clearance.

**MD&A (context).** Recurring revenue was about 84% of total revenue. Instruments and accessories revenue increased 19% to $6.02 billion. Installed base about 11,106 da Vinci systems.
